Returns & Refunds Policy

Handmade pieces deserve a fair, clear returns process — here's exactly how it works.

Return Eligibility

We want you to love what you bring home — but we also know handmade pieces don't always translate perfectly from a photo. You can return most items within 7 days of delivery, provided:

  • The item is unused, unworn, and in its original condition
  • It's returned in its original packaging, including any tags or protective wrapping
  • You have your order ID or the email confirmation for that order

Because every piece is handmade, small variations in glaze, colour, grain, or weave compared to the product photo are normal characteristics of handcraft, not defects — these aren't eligible for return on their own.

Non-Returnable Items

A few categories can't be returned, mainly for hygiene or made-to-order reasons:

  • Earrings and other pierced jewellery, once opened
  • Any item marked "Made to Order" or "Custom" on its product page
  • Gift hampers where the seal has been broken
  • Items marked as final sale during a promotional event

How to Start a Return

  1. Go to Order History in your account and find the order.
  2. Email hello@kheyalkhusi.com with your order ID, the item you'd like to return, and the reason — a quick note is enough.
  3. We'll confirm eligibility and share pickup or drop-off instructions within 1-2 business days.
  4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and hand it over to our courier partner when they arrive.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item (usually within 2-3 business days of pickup), we'll notify you by email. Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days back to your original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are sent via UPI or bank transfer — we'll ask for your details over email.

Exchanges

Prefer a different size, colourway, or piece entirely? Let us know when you request your return and we'll hold your preferred replacement, subject to availability, once the original item is picked up. If there's a price difference, we'll send a payment link or refund the difference accordingly.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or with a genuine manufacturing defect, email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and a clear photo of the issue. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — your choice — and cover all associated costs. This is the one case where we'll always prioritise a fast resolution over back-and-forth.

Return Shipping Costs

For returns due to change of mind, return shipping is generally handled through our courier partner at no cost to you within India; a small pickup convenience fee may apply in a few remote pin codes, which we'll always tell you upfront before you confirm the return. Returns due to our error (damage, defect, wrong item shipped) are always free.

Need help with a specific order?

Our team can look into it directly — just have your order ID handy.
