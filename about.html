Our Story

Two creative minds, handmade little things, and a lot of kheyal — made with care, shared with khusi.

How it started

A little idea, a lot of creativity

KheyalKhushi began in 2026 with two people, a love for creating, and a simple thought — why not turn the things we enjoy making into something that could become special to someone else?

What started with experimenting, learning and making things by hand slowly became KheyalKhushi. From handmade lamps and resin creations to keychains, clocks and ribbon rose bouquets, every piece begins with an idea and is brought to life with care.

We're still at the beginning of our story. We're learning as we go, trying new ideas and putting a little piece of ourselves into everything we create.

Made by hand

Our pieces are created by us, with time, patience and attention to the little details.

Made with care

From choosing materials to adding the finishing touches, we want every piece to be something we're happy to send to you.

Made in Bengal

KheyalKhushi started here in Bengal, carrying a little bit of home, creativity and warmth into everything we make.

What matters to us

The little things we care about

Made, not manufactured

We create in small quantities instead of mass-producing. Every piece gets the time and attention handmade work deserves.

Little imperfections included

Handmade doesn't always mean perfectly identical — and we like it that way. Small variations are part of what makes every creation its own.

Quality over quantity

We're a small team, and we'd rather make fewer things we're proud of than rush through more just for the sake of numbers.

Always experimenting

New colours, materials, designs and slightly random ideas are part of KheyalKhushi. We're still discovering what we can create.

Thoughtful gifting

A gift doesn't have to be huge to mean something. We want to create pieces that feel personal — whether they're for someone you love or simply for yourself.

Growing at our own pace

KheyalKhushi is new. We're learning, improving and building it one creation, one idea and one order at a time.

The story behind our name

Kheyal + Khushi

01 — Kheyal · খেয়াল

It starts with a thought.

An idea, a colour, a shape or one of those random “what if we made this?” moments. That's where most of our creations begin.

02 — Create

Then our hands take over.

We experiment, make, remake and add the little details until an idea starts looking like something we'd genuinely love to keep ourselves.

03 — Khushi · খুশি

The reason we make it.

There's a different kind of happiness in something made by hand — whether you're creating it, gifting it or opening the package yourself.

04 — KheyalKhushi

A little thought, turned into a little joy.

That's what we want this small brand to be — a place for our ideas to become handmade pieces that find their way into your moments, gifts and spaces.
