Our Story

Handmade art and craft from artisans across Bengal — made with kheyal, delivered with khusi.

How it started

A market stall, a promise, and a lot of kheyal

Kheyal Khusi began in 2023 with a single stall at a Kolkata craft mela, selling terracotta pieces from a potter who couldn't get his work in front of anyone beyond his own neighbourhood. He wasn't alone — dozens of artisan families across Bengal were making extraordinary work with nowhere to sell it beyond word of mouth.

"Kheyal" means care, mindfulness — the attention an artisan brings to every piece. "Khusi" means joy — what we hope you feel unwrapping it. That's still the whole idea: connect the two, and get out of the way.

Made by hand, always

No factories, no molds cut short — just skilled hands and time-tested technique.

Fair pay for the maker

Artisans set their own prices — we simply help their work travel further.

Rooted in Bengal

From Kolkata's lanes to villages across the state — every region has a craft of its own.

What we stand for

A few things we won't compromise on

No middlemen markups

Artisans quote their own price. We take a transparent commission to run the platform — never a hidden cut buried in the sticker price.

Genuinely handmade

Every product page tells you exactly who made it and how. If it isn't hand-thrown, hand-stitched, or hand-cast, it isn't on Kheyal Khusi.

Slow, on purpose

Handmade work can't be rushed. We'd rather ship a little later than push an artisan to cut corners on craft they're proud of.

Growth for artisans

We reinvest part of every sale into training, tools, and helping artisan households take on apprentices from their own community.

Only what's real

Natural materials, traditional techniques, no mass-market shortcuts — even when that means a longer wait or a higher price.

A relationship, not a transaction

Most of our artisan partners have been with us since year one. We'd rather grow slowly with people we trust than scale carelessly.

How we got here

A short history

2023

One stall, one potter

Kheyal Khusi starts as a single stall at a Kolkata craft mela, selling terracotta from one artisan family.

2024

Twelve artisan partners

Word spreads through craft networks in Nadia and Bankura. We move online with a small catalogue and local delivery.

2025

Pan-Bengal, pan-India shipping

Partner artisans cross 80, spanning pottery, kantha textile, dokra metalwork, and Madhubani painting. We ship nationwide.

2026

120+ artisans and counting

Today, Kheyal Khusi works with artisan families across 18 districts — and we're just getting started.
