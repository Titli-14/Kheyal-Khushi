Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, and caring for handmade pieces.

Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?
Most orders ship within 2-4 business days, since every piece is checked by hand before it leaves the studio. Delivery within West Bengal usually takes 3-5 days after that; the rest of India typically takes 5-9 days depending on your pin code. You'll get a tracking link by email the moment your order ships.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet — we currently ship only within India. We're working on international shipping for select categories (jewellery and small textile pieces first) and will announce it on our Instagram when it's ready.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
If your order hasn't shipped yet, yes — email us at hello@kheyalkhusi.com with your order ID as soon as possible. Because most pieces are made or finished to order, we can't guarantee changes once an artisan has started preparing your item.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also see the current status of every order anytime under Order History in your account.

Returns & Exchanges

What's your return policy?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for most items, provided they're unused and in their original packaging. Because each piece is handmade, small variations in glaze, grain, or weave are normal and not considered defects. Full details are on our Returns Policy page.
My item arrived damaged — what do I do?
We're sorry — that happens occasionally with fragile handmade pieces in transit. Email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and a photo of the damage, and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund, whichever you prefer.
How long do refunds take?
Once we receive and inspect a returned item, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days back to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or UPI.

Products & Care

Are these products actually handmade?
Yes, every single piece. Each product page names the artisan or artisan family who made it and, where relevant, the technique used — hand-thrown pottery, hand-block-printed textile, hand-cast dokra metalwork, and so on. No mass-produced items are listed on Kheyal Khusi.
Why do pieces from the same collection look slightly different?
That's the nature of handmade work — no two pieces thrown, painted, or woven by hand are ever perfectly identical. We consider that a feature, not a flaw, but if you'd like a closer match to the photos shown, message us before ordering and we'll check with the artisan.
How do I care for pottery and terracotta pieces?
Hand wash with a soft sponge and mild soap — avoid dishwashers and harsh detergents, which can dull glazes over time. Unglazed terracotta is porous, so keep it dry when not in use and avoid prolonged contact with liquids.
How do I care for kantha and handwoven textiles?
Gentle hand wash in cold water with a mild detergent, and dry flat in shade rather than direct sunlight to protect natural dyes. Avoid wringing — press out water gently instead.

Payments & Account

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and net banking through Razorpay, as well as Cash on Delivery for eligible pin codes.
Do I need an account to place an order?
Yes — creating an account lets us keep your order history, saved addresses, and wishlist in one place, and makes checkout faster next time. Signing up takes less than a minute.
Is my payment information safe?
Yes. All payments are processed securely through Razorpay — we never see or store your card, UPI, or banking details on our own servers.
