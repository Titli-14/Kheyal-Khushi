Why Kheyal Khusi

Behind every piece, a pair of hands you'll never see — until now

We work directly with artisan families across West Bengal — potters, embroiderers, metal-casters, and weavers — so their craft reaches your home exactly as they intended it: unhurried, and one of a kind.

Made by hand, always

No factories, no molds cut short — just skilled hands and time-tested technique.

Fair pay for the maker

Artisans set their own prices — we simply help their work travel further.

Rooted in Bengal

From Kolkata's lanes to villages across the state — every region has a craft of its own.
